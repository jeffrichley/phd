## What it establishes

Studying frozen-backbone language agents in ALFWorld and BabyAI, this paper argues that
"external memory does not resolve the continual-learning problem; it reshapes it into a
problem of memory representation and retrieval design." The bottleneck moves from
parameter capacity to context window; interference moves from gradient overwrite to
retrieval pollution. Its named failure modes are retrieval pollution, context competition,
memory dilution, and retrieval diversity collapse. Two findings matter most: abstract,
distilled memories give positive forward transfer where raw trajectories give negative
transfer; and negative transfer concentrates on hard cases: the tasks the agent cannot
already solve are the ones cross-task memory breaks (on one task pair, a 26-point drop on
the hard subset). It also shows the stability–plasticity trade-off re-emerging inside
memory itself: the configuration with the strongest forward transfer also shows the most
severe forgetting in the same run.

## What to pull

- The analytical tools the dissertation adopts: the key/value decomposition of memory into
  representation versus organisation, and the easy-versus-hard subset diagnostic that
  localises where reuse helps and where it hurts (RQ2's analysis plan).
- The design lever: prefer abstract procedural skill representations over raw
  trajectories, and instrument the composition cases specifically: they are the hard
  cases where retrieval noise concentrates.
- The failure modes to measure rather than assume away: a polluted retrieval is a
  misleading scaffold, and whether structure prevents or merely relocates that failure is
  part of what the envelope work must certify (bears on RQ3's false-confidence analysis).

## Where it stops

The study covers two environments, one task pair each, one retriever, and two runs, with
discrete text actions throughout: its authors explicitly choose controlled analysis over
broad coverage and do not claim generality to other domains. Nothing embodied is tested:
no continuous control, no locomotion, no fluid coupling, and no comparison against
weight-update continual learning. That is the opening the dissertation walks through: a
controlled embodied test of whether, for motor skills under fluid coupling, an inspectable
memory avoids the relocated failure modes this paper names, or merely inherits them.
