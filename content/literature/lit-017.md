## What it establishes

PackNet cycles train, prune, retrain, freeze for each task in sequence: after training a
task, the lowest-magnitude fifty to seventy-five percent of that task's weights are set to
zero, a short retraining recovers accuracy, and the survivors are frozen permanently. The
next task trains only the freed weights while reading, but never modifying, everything
frozen before it, so prior-task performance is literally unchanged when a new task is
added. A per-task binary mask selects the active weights at inference. Adding three
fine-grained classification tasks to an ImageNet-trained network lands within one to two
and a half points of individually trained networks, consistently beats
learning-without-forgetting, and costs about a sixteenth of the network's size in masks;
the recipe carries over to residual and densely connected architectures. It is the
canonical parameter-isolation method: stability achieved by making parameters immobile
rather than by regularizing or replaying.

## What to pull

- The parameter-isolation baseline paper 2's continual-learning family requires: the
  modular-in-weights foil, whose forgetting is zero by construction: it anchors the
  no-forgetting corner while the acquisition-cost and transfer comparisons do the work.
- The design accommodation already locked in the methodology: task identity is supplied
  at acquisition so its masks work, while evaluation is task-free, and the gap between
  what PackNet needs and what memory-based methods cope without is itself evidence.
- Its own capacity accounting: roughly three percent added error when a task arrives
  third instead of first, and the recommendation to add the hardest task first, a
  scheduling luxury a lifelong stream cannot grant.

## Where it stops

A skill in PackNet is a binary mask over shared weights: there is no inspectable record
of what the module does, no conditions under which it applies, and nothing that can be
queried or audited. Reuse is wholesale: every later task reads all frozen weights
regardless of relevance, and the pruning fraction is fixed no matter how near or far the
new task is, so the mechanism has no similarity axis and cannot respond to one, let
alone reveal whether transfer benefit is flat or scaling in similarity under fluid
coupling. Free parameters are consumed monotonically until the network is full, the task
label is mandatory at inference, and frozen tasks can never improve from later
experience. Every result is supervised image classification; there is no reinforcement
learning, no dynamics, and no embodiment anywhere in the paper.
