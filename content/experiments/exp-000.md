## Reading

The measurement H1.1 rests on. On the stock pipeline the probe recovers effective mass
equal to dry mass (factor 1.0) on both axes; the specified added-mass reaction is not
realised. With the `lighthill` plugin active, the probe recovers 1.115 axial and 1.345 lateral,
matching the Lamb potential-flow coefficients for the body. The lateral/axial
added-mass ratio (0.345 / 0.115 ≈ 3) is the anisotropy the dissertation's argument
builds on. Deterministic probe; single run by design.
