Colophon

How this record is kept

Everything is published and everyone sees the same thing. There is no logged-in view, no gated section, and nothing held back for the committee — they read the same pages as anyone who follows the link.

Written Markdown, under version control

Every run, note, decision, and section is one file in the repository. Nothing is entered through a browser, so nothing can be quietly amended after the fact; the commit history is the audit trail.

Published Rebuilt on every commit

A commit builds the pages and deploys them as static files. Nothing on the site runs on a server.

Last build —

public.contact

How to reach you, as plain text a reader can copy — institutional address is the right one. Not a form: this site has no way to send mail.

Not indexed Every page carries noindex. Public in the sense that the link works, not in the sense that a search engine will hand it to a stranger.
No accounts Nothing to sign in to and nothing to request. If someone has the link, they already have all of it.
