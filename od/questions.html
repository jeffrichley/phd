Status vocabulary

○Open

Stated, not yet tested. The default. A hypothesis stays open until a pre-registered run has actually reported.

●Supported

Evidence meets the bar set in §1.8, across the seed count you committed to. Cite the run IDs, not the impression.

◑Inconclusive

Ran, but the result does not separate the hypotheses. Say what additional evidence would resolve it.

✕Refuted

The evidence goes the other way. Keep it visible: a refuted hypothesis that was honestly tested is a contribution.

Status is carried by the glyph and the word together; colour is never the only signal.
