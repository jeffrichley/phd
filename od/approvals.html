The four outcomes

Use these four exactly, in content/decisions/*.md. A response that is not one of them is not a decision — file it in §08.

Decision outcomes and what each one means
OutcomeMeans
●Approved Proceed as written.
◑Approved with revisions Proceed once the noted changes land. Requires a comment.
✕Changes requested Do not proceed. Requires a comment.
○Comment only Not a decision, a note for the record.
Why a comment is required “Changes requested” with no reason produces a second meeting to find out what the reason was. Write it in the same commit as the decision, while you still have it in front of you.
Append only Never rewrite a recorded decision. A superseding decision gets its own entry against its own document version; the earlier one stays exactly as it was. That trail is the point of the page.
