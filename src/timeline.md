## The gates

ODU's manuscript-based format makes **submission, not acceptance, the chapter gate** — a
paper under review at defense counts. That sets the two degree gates:

| Gate | Bar | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Candidacy / proposal** | Paper 1 submitted; papers 2–3 with locked questions and methods | Paper 1 at the submission gate; papers 2–3 questions and methods locked |
| **Defense** | All three papers submitted | — |

The candidacy exam sits in the last coursework semester by handbook rule, so coursework
pace — not research — sets the critical path. That is a measured statement: the
simulation platform and the first full paper draft were built from zero in roughly
thirteen days of working time.
